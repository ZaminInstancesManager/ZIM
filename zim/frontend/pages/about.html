About ZIM

Versions

ZIM is the Zamin Instances Manager: a Chrome-inspired desktop application powered by a Rust-based workload-management engine. The shell and the engine ship as one bundle; the engine runs as an independent, separately-recoverable process on your machine.

Updates

Updates are consent-shaped: nothing checks or installs unless you ask, and every payload is minisign-signed before it installs. In a browser view this page shows engine-side facts only — the update channel lives in the desktop app.

The alpha

This is alpha software: real, installable, end-to-end tested — and explicitly not production-ready. What works and what does not is documented in the release notes for this version.
